# Tarot artwork provenance

The 78-card atlas contains authentic Rider–Waite–Smith artwork by Pamela Colman Smith, originally published in 1909–1910 under the direction of Arthur Edward Waite. It replaces the earlier generated deck so the images, suit symbols, and card numbers correspond to the named cards.

Source scans: Wikimedia Commons, the 1910 Pam-A deck. Each source file's description page identifies the artwork as public domain. The accompanying manifest records every source image URL, description-page URL, card index, and source file SHA-256. This records the source's published licensing status; it is not a guarantee regarding rights in every jurisdiction.

The atlas preserves the original scan colors and complete card borders. Scans are resized and arranged into 13 columns × 6 rows, each cell 192 × 330 pixels. No generative reconstruction, invented lettering, or synthesized symbols are used. ELEV8MI's violet interface supplies the surrounding styling.

Order: 22 Major Arcana (Fool through World), then Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles, each Ace through Ten, Page, Knight, Queen, King. Index 12 is named “The Hanged One” in the website and depicts the original “The Hanged Man” card.

Original artwork: Pamela Colman Smith. Direction: Arthur Edward Waite.
