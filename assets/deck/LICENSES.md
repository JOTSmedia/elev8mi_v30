# ELEV8MI Tarot: art sources and licenses

**Card faces:** recolored public-domain 1909–1910 Rider-Waite-Smith art. The source is the original first-edition ("Pam-A") scans of Pamela Colman Smith's illustrations, published under the direction of Arthur Edward Waite (William Rider & Son, London, 1909–1910). Only these original scans from Wikimedia Commons are used. No later copyrighted recolorings or editions (for example the 1971 U.S. Games Systems deck) are used or referenced.

**Public-domain status:** all 78 Commons file pages carry `{{PD-old-auto-expired|deathyear=1942}}` ("Public domain"). Commons gives the date as 1910 and the author as Pamela Colman Smith, and notes that Smith worked for hire and that Waite, the copyright holder, died in 1942. Public Domain Mark: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/ . This records the published status on Commons on 2 Oct 2026. It is not legal advice for every jurisdiction.

Each source file was downloaded from upload.wikimedia.org, and its SHA-256 was checked against the site's `assets/TAROT-SOURCES.json` before processing. All 78 matched.

## JOTS.MEDIA work
The ELEV8MI palette restyle of every scan, the custom ELEV8MI card frame, name plates, numeral medallions, pip-card side mattes and the card back are original work by JOTS.MEDIA (John Baab) for ELEV8MI, 2026. The card back uses the ELEV8MI logo (a client brand asset, not public domain). The scans were recolored with a per-hue palette map that keeps Smith's line work. The drawings, compositions and symbols are Smith's and were not redrawn, retouched or generated. The site's name "The Hanged One" is used for card XII, which shows the original "The Hanged Man" artwork.

## Fonts
Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond (both SIL Open Font License 1.1) are rendered into the images. These are the site's local copies; see `assets/fonts/LICENSES.md`.
