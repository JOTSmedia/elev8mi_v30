## orbit

Create a 2160 x 3840 portrait photographic cinematic environment plate, edge to edge, absolutely no text, no labels, no panels or borders. Vertical composition is essential: upper 50 percent is a deep indigo-black space field with extremely fine realistic stars and a subtle photographic Milky Way, restrained natural violet interstellar dust, lots of dark negative space; between 50 and 68 percent Earth's enormous curved horizon enters the frame with a razor thin pale cyan atmosphere, realistic blue planetary cloud texture seen from low orbit; lower 32 percent becomes pale blue high atmosphere filled with richly detailed silvery storm clouds and a faint violet twilight tint, clouds run out of the bottom edge to join a second plate. NASA-quality realism, realistic exposure, immense scale, no painted dots, no artificial lens flare, no cartoon sun or moon, no bright fantasy nebula. Detailed photographic clouds and planet. Keep features across the width, including both edges, and a clean central field for web copy. Restrained cool blue / violet dusk palette. Output only the scenery.

## subterranean

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: tall continuous scrolling website background, final third of a descent below a real forest floor. Create a 2160 x 3840 portrait cinematic photographic environment plate, edge to edge, no text, labels, panels, borders or infographic styling. Vertical composition: upper 18 percent starts with a close forest floor, weathered fallen wood, lush moss, damp dark earth, ferns at sides, with fine branching tree roots hanging from its underside; next 30 percent scans down through a shadowy natural limestone sinkhole, exposed roots embedded in brown sediment, realistic soil layers and coarse porous mineral rock, the opening narrows overhead. Lower 52 percent reveals the interior of a vast authentic subterranean limestone cave, intricate natural stalactites, rough stratified rock, a still dark teal underground pool at the very bottom, an understated shaft of cool moonlight from a tiny distant opening, believable very low ambient reflected light that still reveals rock detail. It should feel like a camera physically descending beneath the forest into a real cave, an atmospheric photographic composite, never a textbook cutaway or tiled texture. Rich physical detail and beautiful depth; geology is the subject, not magical props. Neutral charcoal, earthy brown, subtle cool blue and faint natural violet reflections. No glowing crystals, no lava, no people, no fantasy, no CGI or plastic surfaces. Scenery across both edges with dark central usable area for web content. Output only scenery.
