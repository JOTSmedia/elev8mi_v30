## Tuning (528 Hz)
Equal temperament with **A4 = 444 Hz → C5 = 528.0 Hz, C4 = 264 Hz** (tonal center C). Verified by FFT:
isolated C5/A4 notes rendered exactly as used in each loop (above), plus a whole-track FFT. All
measured C5 values are within ±5 cents (±1.5 Hz) of 528 Hz; the bowed strings read a little wider
on the whole-track FFT because of natural vibrato. Strongest spectral peaks of the guitar track sit
a median 1.0 cent from the A444 grid vs 16 cents from A440.

## Loudness
All six tracks are mastered to **−18 LUFS integrated (EBU R128)** with true peak under −1 dBTP
(circular look-ahead limiter, so the loop stays seamless). Switching tracks needs no volume change.

## Looping
Each track is rendered into a circular 120 s buffer: note decays and reverb tails wrap into the start,
so end→start is seamless. For gap-free looping in browsers prefer Web Audio (`decodeAudioData` +
`source.loop = true`) or the OGG; plain `<audio loop>` with MP3 can add a tiny gap in some browsers
due to MP3 encoder padding.

Masters and regeneration scripts (JOTS.MEDIA): `synth_guitar.py`, `make_set.py`, `elev8lib.py`,
`master_all.py`, `verify_all.py`, `verify_notes.py`.
